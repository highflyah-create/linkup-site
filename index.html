How it works

Every plan has a life.

From the first idea to the final photo, your event flows through four natural phases.

Create a space. Add friends. Sort the details with chat, checklists, and shared decisions.

Step 02

Countdown begins. Excitement builds. Final details click into place as the day approaches.
