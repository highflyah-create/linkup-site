How it works

Every plan has a life.

A LinkUp moves through four phases on its own, and the app changes with it, so everyone always knows where a plan stands.

Planning

Sort the details

Pick a date, see who’s in and chat it through in one place.

Coming up

The day gets close

The plan moves up your list and everyone can see it’s on.

Live

It’s happening

The meetup map opens and photos start landing in one shared place.

Memory

It lives on

Afterwards the event becomes a memory the whole group can come back to.

Here’s one night out, from the first message to the memory. One moment from each phase.

01Planning

Turn the chat into a plan.

Someone drops an idea in the group, and instead of fifty messages going nowhere, it becomes a LinkUp. The event card sits right in the conversation, so everyone can see the details and who’s going.

  • Group and one-to-one chat with photos, replies and reactions
  • Create a LinkUp straight from the conversation
02Coming up

Know what’s on and who’s in.

Going, Maybe or Can’t go. Every plan wears its phase colour, so one glance tells you what’s being planned, what’s coming up and what’s happening right now.

  • Filter by live, coming up or still planning
  • Hosts and co-hosts keep each plan on track
03Live

Find each other on the night.

No more “where are you?” texts. The meetup map opens 30 minutes before the start, so you can see everyone heading in and set a spot to meet.

  • Share your live location, or just type where you are
  • Drop a meet pin everyone can get directions to
  • Sharing stops by itself once everyone has arrived
04Memory

Keep the memory.

Afterwards, the event becomes a memory with everyone’s photos and the best of the chat, plus a few fun highlights, like who took the most pictures and who stayed out latest.

  • Everyone’s photos from the night in one place
  • A memory the whole group can come back to

Features

Everything a plan needs.

The chat you already use for plans, with the parts it was always missing.

Real messaging

Group and one-to-one chats with photos, videos, replies, reactions and read receipts.

Event cards

Every plan gets a card in the chat with the time, the place and who’s going, one tap from the details.

RSVPs that count

Going, Maybe or Can’t go, with a clear list of who’s coming and hosts to keep it moving.

Meetup map

See who’s on the way, set a meet pin and get directions, only around the time of the event.

Shared photos

Everyone’s photos land in one place for the event, so nobody has to chase pictures the next day.

Memories

Past events turn into memory cards with the photos, the stats and a few highlights from the night.

Privacy

Your location, on your terms.

Location sharing in LinkUp is built for one job, getting your group together, and it switches itself off when that job is done.

Only your event can see you

Only members of the event see where you are, and only until the meetup ends.

Stops when everyone’s arrived

The moment the whole group is there, sharing turns off for everyone.

Rather not share? Type it

Tell the group where you are in a few words instead of sharing your position.

You stay in control

Block or report anyone, export your data, or delete your account from settings.
